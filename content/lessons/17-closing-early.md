> Holding to expiry is rarely the best use of a trade. Most of the fee is earned early, while most of the risk stays to the end. Knowing **when to close early** is the third adjustment.

## The app's own early exits

- **Profit exit:** once 90% of the premium has decayed, the whole group closes. The last 10% is not worth the same risk.
- **Time exit:** every leg closes when fewer than 7 days are left, before physical delivery and rising margins.

::visual theta-decay

## When to close earlier yourself

- **Results or a dividend** are coming before expiry and you didn't plan for them. Close, or add protection.
- **Your reason is gone.** The setup you sold no longer fits the rules (the delta has grown well past 0.15, or support has broken).
- **Half or more of the fee is already earned in the first third of the time.** Taking it and re-entering later often beats waiting.

## What closing early is not

- It is not closing winners fast and holding losers. That is the habit that empties accounts.
- It is not a reason to skip the stop-loss. If the stop is hit, it decides.

## A simple routine

Each week, look at every open group and ask: would I open this trade today, at these prices? If the answer is no, close it.
